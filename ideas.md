# HOLOFLUX Design Directions

## Three possible approaches

### 1. Orbital Observatory
**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained dark-space laboratory with precise instrument typography, spectral light, and depth created by the simulated universe rather than by decorative interface glow. It should feel like using an elite observatory in orbit.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Liquid Chromatics
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, gallery-like generative-art environment whose particles are presented as illuminated ink and sculptural colour. The mood is tactile, intimate, and expressive rather than technical.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Signal Archive
**Very Brief Intro:** A sober archival-science visual system based on monochrome data cards, measurement ticks, and sparse scan-line motion. The particle world provides the contrast to its deliberately analytical controls.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Orbital Observatory

### Design Movement
**Contemporary scientific instrumentation** with the restraint of Apple hardware interfaces, the typographic clarity of NASA mission control graphics, and cinematic deep-space imaging. The particle field is the hero; the interface stays surgically quiet.

### Core Principles
1. **The universe, not the chrome, creates spectacle.** Glowing particles and physical movement create drama; controls avoid gratuitous neon framing.
2. **Information is layered by urgency.** Core state stays in the perimeter, deeper configuration lives in a collapsible instrument bay, and progressive onboarding reveals one action at a time.
3. **Physics should be legible.** Force fields appear as thin radial vectors, restrained rings, and micro-labels that clarify cause and effect.
4. **Every UI element earns its footprint.** Large screens preserve the spatial canvas; small screens promote an uncluttered touch-first dock.

### Color Philosophy
Near-black graphite and midnight blue provide true visual recess, allowing **Arctic Cyan** to indicate live interaction and soft pearl white to carry information. Physical events introduce temporary restrained spectral colour (coral for collision, violet for vortex), never permanent rainbow noise. The canvas has depth through a blue-black vignette and smoke-like translucent nebula layers instead of gradients on cards.

### Layout Paradigm
The particle canvas is a full-bleed **instrument field**. Readouts cling to the top edge as an asymmetric constellation: identity at upper left and systems telemetry at upper right. The lower dock is a horizontal command rail. A side instrument bay slides over the far right only when configuration is deliberate. This keeps the center intentionally empty for spatial interaction.

### Signature Elements
1. A crisp four-spoke **flux reticle** that follows active hands and virtual pointer forces.
2. Fine orbital guide arcs and coordinate ticks around active force fields.
3. A thin segmented **event pulse line** that briefly appears along the bottom when a collision, galaxy seed, or supernova fires.

### Interaction Philosophy
Controls respond like laboratory hardware: decisive press feedback, no ornamental bounce, and brief local feedback in the form of event chips. Gestures produce measurable labels such as `ATTRACT · 74%`; selections use quiet color changes and micro-scale motion.

### Animation
Canvas effects evolve continuously through the simulation. UI enters with a 220ms custom ease-out and leaves with 160ms opacity-and-transform transitions. Panels translate 14px from their triggering edge, never pop or spin. The boot sequence uses timed type and line illumination; reduced-motion mode disables nonessential overlay transitions and particle trails while preserving state changes.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for functional display text, control labels, and numerical telemetry; use **DM Mono** for machine values and transient events. Headlines use Space Grotesk 600–700 with modest letter-spacing; small labels use DM Mono uppercase at 10–11px with 0.14em tracking. No generic sci-fi display fonts are used.

### Brand Essence
**HOLOFLUX is a browser-native spatial laboratory for people who want to sculpt living particle physics with their hands.**

Personality: **precise, cinematic, alive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, sensory, and instrument-like; CTAs are calm imperatives; microcopy tells the truth about technical state.

Example lines:

> Your hand is now a force.

> Camera frames stay on this device.

### Wordmark & Logo
The HOLOFLUX mark is a compact four-point flux aperture: two interrupted orbital rings crossing a centered source point. The wordmark uses a custom-spaced Space Grotesk treatment with a split `O` suggesting field lines. It is never rendered as a default-font logo.

### Signature Brand Color
**Arctic Cyan — #79F3FF.** A high-luminance cool cyan reserved for live tracking, active controls, and the core of the force field.
